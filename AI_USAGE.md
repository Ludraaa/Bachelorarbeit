# AI Usage Statement

AI tools were used in a limited and assistive manner during the development of the software accompanying this bachelor's thesis.

Specifically, I used Claude Sonnet 4.6 through the web interface.

AI assistance was mainly used for debugging, refactoring, limited implementation, and other development tasks. Where AI was used for implementation, I generally specified the intended changes and their integration into the existing code, rather than delegating the design or solution of a problem. The design and overall approach of the implemented components, as well as the scientific reasoning and evaluation, were carried out by me.

AI assistance was used for the following tasks:

* parts of the linker and knowledge base modules,
* file metadata infrastructure,
* debug logging infrastructure,
* plotting and visualization of evaluation results,
* selected refactoring and code organization,
* development of the result explorer website,
* pytest infrastructure,
* Makefile and Dockerfile infrastructure.

AI-generated code was reviewed, adapted, and tested by me before being included in the repository. AI was not used to carry out the scientific analysis or to interpret the experimental results.

I am responsible for the final code and the contents of the thesis.
